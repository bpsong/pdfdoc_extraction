## What changed

The combined guides were separated into Operator, Admin, Architecture,
Developer and IT Support reading paths with shared technical references.
Operator/Admin pages use screen-based instructions and explain unfamiliar
terms. Installation, commands, recovery and exact contracts have dedicated
technical pages. No application behaviour was changed by this reorganisation.

Following Admin feedback, the maintained Admin guides were expanded to restore
practical depth: detailed review-form controls and nested examples, extraction
method tradeoffs (including LiteParse versus GLM-OCR for larger documents), a
complete built-in task guide, a worked pipeline setup, acceptance testing and
watch-folder rollout. The simplified initial wording is superseded by those
expanded guides; the original dated snapshot remains unchanged.

The Operator guides were then reviewed against the original Operator section,
FAQ, manual-split instructions and current production screens. Restored detail
includes upload limits and uncertain-acceptance recovery, queue filters and
ownership, field/row correction, source/confidence interpretation, draft and
completion handling, split-plan examples, result interpretation and failure
investigation. Current-screen clarifications distinguish original extracted
values from PDF evidence, explain care with unsaved edits when reclaiming a
review, and identify the file list as filenames rather than download controls.

## Who is affected and what to do

Start at [the documentation entrance](../../README.md) and choose your audience.
The previous document paths and section links now lead through navigation
pages. Repository task standards and the config-check CLI references retain
their existing authoritative locations.

## Historical preservation

Every original document and audit image is preserved byte-for-byte in the
[2026-10-10 snapshot](../archive/2026-10-10/README.md). Its manifest records
original path, archived path, byte length and SHA-256. Dated reading copies of
audits, validations and measurements sit under History and identify their
original checkpoint. Earlier guide history is [retained separately](user-guide-history.md).

## Maintenance

Maintained pages carry audience, owner, status and verification basis. Their
review date reflects a repository/source review, not a newly performed live
provider or deployment check. Record future user-visible/operational changes
centrally; keep ordinary editing history in Git.
